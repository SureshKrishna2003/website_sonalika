Privacy Policy

At Sonalika Photography, we prioritize your privacy and ensure your personal data is handled with maximum security and care.

1. Information Collection

We collect personal information such as client names, phone numbers, WhatsApp contact info, email addresses, and event details strictly for booking management and service fulfillment.

2. Image Rights & Confidentiality

Photographs captured during shoots are kept confidential. Select portfolio feature images are published only with explicit client consent. Your media is never sold or distributed to unapproved third parties.

3. Data Security & Archives

High-resolution edited galleries are backed up in private cloud archives for up to 12 months post-event for safe client download.

4. Privacy Contact

For any privacy requests or data removal inquiries, please contact us directly via phone or WhatsApp at +91 93455 78780.

Terms & Conditions

Terms of service governing all photography bookings and commissioned work with Sonalika Photography.

1. Booking Deposit

Event dates are secured upon receipt of an advance booking deposit. Advance deposits reserve the team specifically for your chosen dates.

2. Creative Direction

Sonalika Photography retains creative control over photography style, framing, lighting, and post-production color curation reflecting our signature narrative aesthetic.

3. Delivery Timelines

Highlight teasers are delivered within 7 business days. Full edited high-res digital galleries are delivered within 30 business days following the event.

4. Usage Rights

Clients receive personal rights to print, share, and publish delivered images online. Commercial reproduction requires prior written authorization.
